# Booking terms and data-use notice — review draft

Version 0.2 — November 4, 2026

**Non-binding review draft.** The documents below are proposed wording for a real accommodation business. They are not in force and do not create a booking contract or activate the services described. This preview makes no real reservation, charge or email delivery. Operator details, contact channels, rate conditions and data-handling arrangements require approval before publication.

## A. Booking terms

### 1. Your agreement with Aavana Estate

These terms apply to accommodation booked directly through Aavana Estate. Read them together with the accommodation description, price breakdown and rate conditions displayed before payment. Your confirmation records the dates, accommodation types, guest allocation and accepted total for your booking. Keep a copy of these details and contact us promptly if anything appears incorrect.

### 2. Booking contact and guest allocation

The person making the booking must be at least 18 and authorised to provide the booking details. Please provide an accurate name and email address; a phone number is optional. Each room or villa must include at least one adult. Adults are 18 or older. All guests, including children and infants, count toward the stated capacity. Extra beds, meals and additional services are included only where expressly listed in the accepted offer.

### 3. Stay dates and accommodation

A booking uses one arrival date and one departure date for all selected accommodation. Stays are between 1 and 14 nights, subject to availability. Dates follow local time in India; departure is not an additional chargeable night. Booking an accommodation type does not allocate a specific numbered room. Arrival and departure arrangements will be stated in the booking information supplied before confirmation.

### 4. Prices, taxes and availability

Catalogue prices are indicative until you obtain a quote for your dates and selection. The review shows quantities, nightly charges, included GST and the total payable. GST shown as included is not an additional charge on top of that total. Quotes remain valid for 15 minutes and do not hold accommodation. If a quote expires, availability changes or the price changes before confirmation, you must review the revised offer before proceeding. An accepted confirmation retains its recorded price breakdown.

### 5. Payment and confirmation

Your booking is confirmed only when the reservation is recorded and a confirmation reference is issued. A payment decline does not create a booking. If the result is interrupted or uncertain, use the existing retry or status-check option before submitting another payment attempt. Do not assume an interrupted response means payment failed. Booking correspondence uses the email address you provide. If an expected confirmation does not arrive, check your spam folder and contact booking support with your reference. Treat the confirmation link as private because it provides access to stay and price details. A booking confirmation is separate from any tax invoice.

### 6. Changes, cancellation and refunds

The cancellation, amendment, no-show and refund conditions for your selected rate must be disclosed before payment and form part of the accepted offer. Contact booking support to request a change or cancellation; a request takes effect only after it is acknowledged and agreed. Availability and any price difference will be explained before a change is accepted. Nothing in these terms makes every booking non-refundable or limits any refund or remedy required by applicable law.

### 7. Your stay and assistance

Guests must observe the property rules supplied before booking and take reasonable care of the accommodation and other guests. Do not assume that photographs or amenity descriptions include services that are not expressly offered. Contact booking support before confirming if you need clarification about accessibility, arrival arrangements or a particular facility. Any additional charge must be disclosed and agreed or otherwise lawfully due.

### 8. Questions and your rights

For booking questions, corrections or complaints, contact [booking support contact to be supplied] and include your reference where available. We will explain any proposed resolution and any information needed to investigate. These terms do not exclude mandatory consumer rights, statutory remedies or access to a competent court or consumer authority.

## B. Data-use notice

### Who is responsible

Aavana Estate uses the information described here to manage direct accommodation bookings. The responsible operator is [legal operator name and postal address to be supplied]. Contact [privacy and grievance contact to be supplied] for questions about this notice or your personal information.

### Information we use

We use your booking contact's name and email, an optional phone number, arrival and departure dates, selected accommodation, adult and child counts, booking reference and price records. We also use records needed to resolve duplicate or interrupted submissions and protect the booking service. This form does not ask for children's names, identity documents, card numbers or banking passwords. Please provide only the information requested and ensure you are authorised to share it.

### Why we use it

We use these details to process your booking request, validate guest capacity, record the accepted offer, manage availability, provide confirmation and essential booking correspondence, answer questions and resolve booking issues. Relevant records may also be used to meet applicable legal, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements. An optional phone number is used for booking-related contact. Booking acceptance is not permission for promotional messages; any marketing choice must be offered separately.

### Storage and service providers

An unfinished booking draft is stored in your browser tab so you can resume it. Submitted booking records are stored by the booking service. Hosting, database and any approved correspondence providers may process information for these purposes under appropriate arrangements. Access is restricted to people and providers who need it for their role. Before this notice takes effect, the operator must disclose the applicable provider and processing-location details, including any arrangements involving processing outside India.

### Confirmation links and your choices

Anyone holding a confirmation link may be able to view the accommodation, dates, guest counts and recorded prices. Contact details are excluded from that public reference view. Keep the link private and avoid sharing it in public messages. You may leave the phone field blank. Required contact details are needed to process and communicate about a booking; without them we may be unable to complete the request. An email address supplied in a form is not proof of ownership or guaranteed delivery.

### Retention and safeguards

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described, including applicable accounting requirements, legal obligations and unresolved disputes. When retention is no longer justified, records should be deleted or de-identified in accordance with the operator's approved retention and backup schedule. The final schedule must identify the relevant periods before publication. We use safeguards appropriate to the information and service; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

### Requests, withdrawal and concerns

You may contact the privacy contact above to request access to or correction of your information, seek erasure where available, withdraw consent for processing that relies on it, or raise a concern. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully carried out and may affect our ability to provide a requested service. Information subject to a legal retention obligation may need to be kept. We will explain applicable limitations and the available grievance or escalation route. Your rights and remedies under applicable law remain unaffected.

## C. Acceptance wording for review

Both choices are separate and initially unchecked. Offer both full documents before submission; closing a document does not accept it.

1. I am 18 or older and accept the booking terms.
2. I agree to the use of my booking details to process and manage this booking, as explained in the data-use notice.

## D. Owner checklist — not part of the guest documents

Before publication, supply the legal operator identity and address, working booking/privacy/grievance contacts, verified arrival arrangements and property rules, and the actual rate-specific cancellation/amendment/no-show/refund terms. Approve provider/location disclosures, retention and backup periods, access controls and an operational rights/request process. Confirm the legal basis for each processing purpose; two checkboxes alone do not establish legal compliance. No fixed deletion or refund deadline has been invented.

The current website has test payments and no transactional email. This draft proposes future-facing wording; it does not enable either service. The confirmation mock shows a labelled example of the desired email-sent message beside its preview link. No download is offered. Real email delivery and a production payment service require separate work; production must not present an example message as a delivery result.

## E. Source notes

The draft draws on project booking rules and official privacy sources, without claiming certified compliance. Check launch-date applicability and have the completed business-specific documents reviewed before publication. India's DPDP framework has phased commencement; it should not be treated as fully operative merely because a notice has been drafted.

- [DPDP Act 2023, MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf), especially sections 5–6.
- [DPDP Rules 2025, official Gazette hosted by MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), especially rules 1 and 3.

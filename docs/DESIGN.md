# Visual direction

Aavana Estate is a fictional coffee-estate resort demo. The layout leads with a forest photograph, a quiet invitation, and a direct route to rooms. Warm paper, dark woodland ink, hairline dividers, serif headings and plain sans-serif body text form the initial system. No animation or external font downloads are required.

Hallmark Atelier was the closest dealt reference; the approved restrained resort direction overrides its decorative texture and animation suggestions. The scaffold helper failed with "no header block" on the installed reference, so the tokens are authored in globals.css. Revisit visual choices with the owner before expanding day-three pages.

Photography: illustrative Unsplash photos, not representations of the fictional property's actual rooms. Source and license: https://unsplash.com/license . IDs are recorded in src/db/sample-rooms.ts and src/app/page.tsx. Verify asset provenance before a real client launch.
